THE FOUNDER

Amrit Kaur

Bio

Amrit Kaur is a performance coach and founder of OS, a coaching practice focused on strength, conditioning, recovery, and long-term athletic development.

Her coaching philosophy was shaped through years of working with high-performing professionals, athletes, and individuals rebuilding from injury, burnout, and physical limitation. Drawing from backgrounds in boxing, running, strength training, and movement-based performance, her approach emphasizes structure, adaptability, and sustainability over short-term intensity.

Amrit's work integrates performance training with recovery systems, movement quality, and lifestyle management to help clients build strength without sacrificing long-term capacity. Her coaching is rooted in the belief that high performance is not created through exhaustion, but through intelligent structure, consistency, and resilience.

Through OS, she works with clients seeking a more individualized and thoughtful approach to training, one built around longevity, discipline, and the art of the rebuild.
